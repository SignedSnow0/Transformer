# Full English–Italian Tatoeba corpus

Generated by `python scripts/prepare_datasets.py tatoeba`.

- Output: `eng-it-full.tsv` (239,245 `english<TAB>italian` rows)
- Source: https://object.pouta.csc.fi/OPUS-Tatoeba/v2026-07-08/moses/en-it.txt.zip
- Version: OPUS Tatoeba `v2026-07-08`, Moses package
- Source SHA-256: `cc2549ab5b38f4bf74adee586ef24104e00d8d627ae2c981969188a135358d8f`
- License: [CC BY 2.0 FR](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/)
- Terms: <https://tatoeba.org/eng/terms_of_use>

The corpus quality filters are applied: both sides are 12–180 characters and
3–18 words, contain no URLs, email addresses, or markup characters, and differ
from each other. English sentences are unique case-insensitively. With
`--limit N`, a deterministic reservoir sample of seed 42 is used before sorting.

Please cite: J. Tiedemann, 2012. *Parallel Data, Tools and Interfaces in OPUS.*
LREC 2012.
